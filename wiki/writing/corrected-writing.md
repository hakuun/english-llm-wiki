## [2026-05-25] Initial self-introduction sample

### Learner text

hello, my name is alex chen, 30 years old, i am a software developer and worked 5 years starting in 2020, now i am working in technology company in Jiangsu provice, Nanjing city, China. i want prove my english level to CEFR-B2 spend a year, and i need Authoritative and Effective Learning Materials, please tell me how to find this materials.

### Corrected version

Hello, my name is Alex Chen. I am 30 years old. I am a software developer, and I have been working since 2020. Now I work for a technology company in Nanjing, Jiangsu Province, China. I want to improve my English to CEFR B2 within one year, and I need authoritative and effective learning materials. Please tell me how to find these materials.

### More natural version

Hello, my name is Alex Chen. I am 30 years old and work as a software developer at a technology company in Nanjing, Jiangsu Province, China. I have been working in software development since 2020. My goal is to improve my English to CEFR B2 within one year. I need authoritative and effective learning materials, so I would like to know how to find them.

### Key learning points

- Capitalize names, places, nationalities, and the first word of a sentence.
- Use `I am 30 years old`, not `my name is ..., 30 years old`.
- Use `I have been working since 2020` for work experience that started in the past and continues now.
- Use `improve my English to CEFR B2`, not `prove my English level to CEFR-B2`.
- Use `within one year`, not `spend a year` in this sentence.
- Use `these materials`, not `this materials`, because `materials` is plural.
